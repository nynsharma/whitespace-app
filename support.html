My photos don’t appear

Open Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Whitespace and choose Full Access. With Limited Access, only the photos you selected are shown.

A photo won’t open

If it’s stored in iCloud, check your internet connection so it can download.

Where are my exports?

In the Photos app, named like IMG_1234_Whitespace.jpg. Turn on Settings › Hide photos made with Whitespace to keep them out of the library view.

The wall preview doesn’t start

Allow camera access, point at a plain, well-lit wall and move your iPhone slowly until the wall is detected, then tap.

I have a promo code

Open the App Store, tap your profile picture, choose Redeem Gift Card or Code and enter it.
