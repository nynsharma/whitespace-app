Privacy Policy

Whitespace: Photo Frames · Last updated 25 November 2026

Whitespace does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no advertising.

Your photos

With your permission, Whitespace reads your photo library on your iPhone so you can choose photos and videos to frame, and saves framed copies back to your library. Your photos never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

Camera

The camera is used only for the “See on your wall” preview. Nothing is recorded unless you tap the capture button, which saves a picture to your own photo library.

Camera data and location

Exports keep your photo’s camera data (camera, lens, exposure) if you choose. Location data is removed from exports unless you turn on “Keep location”.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences, saved looks and gear list are stored only on your iPhone and are removed when you delete the app.
